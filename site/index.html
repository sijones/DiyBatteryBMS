DIY Battery BMS

Reads a Victron Smart Shunt — over VE.Direct, Bluetooth or MQTT — and speaks Pylontech CAN to your inverter, so a DIY LiFePO4 pack can be charged, monitored and protected like a supported battery. No per-cell BMS wiring: just the shunt you already have.

Set it up from a browser, watch live charge state on its own dashboard, and hook it into Home Assistant over MQTT for automations like off-peak force charging.

Flash a board over USB

This browser cannot talk to a board

Plug an ESP32 in and this reads back what it is — chip, flash size, PSRAM, partition layout — then writes the build that matches it and sets it up on your WiFi. No drivers, no esptool, no zip to unpack.

Nothing is written until you have picked a build and confirmed the details. Your settings are kept where the build allows it — it says which before it starts — and can be saved to your PC first.
