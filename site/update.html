Download firmware.bin

Then, on the device

  1. Open its web interface and go to the Firmware tab.
  2. Under Firmware image, choose the firmware.bin you just downloaded.
  3. Press Update Firmware and wait for it to restart.

Your settings live in NVS, which an over-the-air update never touches — they are kept.

This build is larger than the 1.25MB app slot used before v3.0. If your device still reports the pre-3.0 partition table on the Firmware tab, this update will not fit and has to be written over USB instead — the browser flasher does that, and keeps your settings.

Which build do you need?

Pick the board this firmware is going on. If you are not sure, the browser flasher reads it off the chip for you.
